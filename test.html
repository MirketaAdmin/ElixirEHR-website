The Enterprise AI Problem

Why Enterprises Struggle to Get AI Right

The barriers to successful AI adoption are well-documented yet most organizations still encounter them. Understanding these challenges is the first step to overcoming them.

HIGH RISK

Where Do We Even Start?

With hundreds of AI tools, frameworks, and vendors competing for attention, enterprise leaders struggle to identify which AI investments align with their specific business goals and which are just hype.

No clear AI strategy or prioritized roadmap
HIGH RISK

Fragmented Data, Broken Pipelines

AI is only as powerful as the data feeding it. Most enterprises operate with siloed data across CRM, ERP, and legacy systems making it nearly impossible to build reliable AI models without first fixing the data foundation.

Poor data quality, accessibility, and governance
HIGH RISK

Which Use Cases Are Actually Ready?

Not every AI use case is equally feasible or valuable. Enterprises often invest in complex AI projects when simpler automation could deliver faster ROI or pursue low-impact use cases while missing transformational opportunities.

No framework to evaluate use case feasibility vs. impact

MEDIUM RISK

The Talent and Culture Gap

Building AI capabilities requires more than hiring data scientists. It demands a culture of experimentation, cross-functional AI literacy, and leadership that champions data-driven decision-making at every level.

Skill gaps, cultural resistance, and lack of AI champions

Governance Without a Framework

Deploying AI without clear governance exposes enterprises to regulatory penalties, reputational damage, and ethical blind spots. Most organizations lack the policies, audit trails, and accountability structures AI demands.

The Assessment Framework

Mirketa's 6-Dimension AI Readiness Framework

A holistic, enterprise-grade assessment that evaluates every dimension of your AI readiness not just technology, but strategy, data, people, and governance.

Business Alignment
Strategy & Vision

We evaluate how clearly AI connects to your strategic objectives, the depth of executive sponsorship, and whether your organization has a coherent AI vision that drives prioritization decisions.

  • AI strategy clarity and roadmap
  • Executive buy-in and C-suite alignment
  • Business-driven use case prioritization
  • KPIs and success metrics definition
Data Readiness
Foundation & Quality

AI models are only as reliable as the data they learn from. We assess your data quality, accessibility, governance practices, and integration across enterprise systems.

  • Data quality, completeness, and consistency
  • Integration across CRM, ERP, EHR systems
  • Data governance and privacy compliance
  • Master data management maturity
Technology Landscape
Infrastructure & Stack

We examine whether your existing technology infrastructure can support AI at scale from cloud readiness to API architecture and compatibility with modern AI/ML platforms.

  • Cloud readiness (AWS, Azure, GCP, Salesforce)
  • Legacy system modernization status
  • API architecture and integration capability
  • AI/ML tooling and platform compatibility
Process & Operations
Efficiency & Automation

AI delivers the greatest value in well-defined, repeatable processes. We identify automation opportunities, assess process maturity, and evaluate organizational readiness for AI-driven change.

  • Workflow efficiency and automation potential
  • Process documentation and standardization
  • Scalability of core business operations
  • Change management readiness
Talent & Culture
People & Capability

Technology alone cannot drive transformation people do. We assess AI/ML skill availability, leadership commitment to innovation, and the cultural readiness to embrace AI-driven ways of working.

  • AI/ML skill availability and gaps
  • Culture of data-driven decision-making
  • Training, reskilling, and upskilling plans
  • AI champions and center of excellence
Governance & Risk
Compliance & Ethics

Responsible AI adoption requires clear governance structures, ethical guidelines, and proactive risk management. We evaluate your readiness to deploy AI in a compliant, transparent, and accountable manner.

  • Ethical AI policies and decision transparency
  • Regulatory compliance (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC2)
  • Risk identification and mitigation strategies
  • AI audit trails and accountability frameworks

Not All AI Use Cases Are Created Equal
